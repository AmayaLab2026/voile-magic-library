## Library Classification & Call Number System

Voile Magic Library implements a dual cataloging scheme, pairing our proprietary **Voile Magic Library Classification (VMLC)** system with standardized Library of Congress (LCC) and Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC) systems.

### Call Number Construction Formula

$$\text{VML-}[\text{LCC Subject Class}]\text{.}[\text{Cutter Author No}]\text{-}[\text{Year}]$$

* **VML**: Voile Magic Library repository code.
* **LCC Subject Class**: Library of Congress Classification letter-number combination (e.g., `KF3890` for US food and drug law; `TL781` for aeronautics and astronautics history).
* **Cutter Author Number**: Cutter two-letter/number sequence based on author surname (`.S58` for Shulgin; `.P56` for Pioneers collection).
* **Year of Publication**: The original publication or translated release year.

* **Author**: Alexander T. Shulgin, Ph.D. (1925–2014)
* **Title**: *Controlled Substances: A Chemical and Legal Guide to the Federal Drug Laws* (Second Edition)
* **Publisher**: Ronin Publishing (Berkeley, California, 1992)
* **Call Number**: `VML-KF3890.S58-1992`
* **LCC**: `KF3890 .S58 1992` (Substance Control · Law & Chemical Regulation)
* **DDC**: `344.730545` (United States Public Health Law · Controlled Substances)
* **ISBN**: `0-914171-50-X`
* **Format**: 436 pages, complete unabridged digital translation, with 33 core chemical structure plates.

### Author & Contributor Biographies

#### Principal Author: Alexander "Sasha" Shulgin, Ph.D. (1925–2014)
* **Biographical Sketch**: Preeminent American medicinal chemist, pharmacologist, and toxicologist. Educated at Harvard and UC Berkeley, former senior research chemist at Dow Chemical, and long-time lecturer at UC Berkeley and UCSF.
* **Scientific Contributions**: Discovered Zectran (first biodegradable carbamate pesticide), synthesized and evaluated over 230 novel psychoactive phenethylamines and tryptamines, and co-authored the seminal works *PiHKAL* and *TiHKAL*. Held an unprecedented DEA Schedule I analytical license for decades.

#### Foreword Contributor: Ronald K. Siegel, Ph.D.
* **Role**: Associate Research Professor of Psychiatry and Biobehavioral Sciences, Neuropsychiatric Institute, UCLA School of Medicine. Consultant to the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA).
* **Perspective**: Explores the evolution of pharmacological terminology and judicial classifications from clinical neuropsychopharmacology.

#### Foreword Contributor: Gary L. Henderson, Ph.D.
* **Role**: Associate Professor of Pharmacology and Toxicology, UC Davis School of Medicine.
* **Perspective**: Highlights analytical chromatography (GC-MS), forensic toxicology validation, and structural ambiguity problems in legislative drafting.

#### Foreword Contributor: David P. Krivitsky, Esq.
* **Role**: Prominent California criminal defense attorney and forensic legal specialist.
* **Perspective**: Dissects constitutional due process issues, vagueness doctrines, and the judicial quagmire created by the Federal Analogue Act of 1986.

## Special Collection Vol. 02: Pioneers of Rocket Technology

### Master Bibliographic Record

* **Editor-in-Chief**: Dr. T. M. Mel'kumov et al. (USSR Academy of Sciences)
* **Pioneering Authors**: N. I. Kibal'chich, Yu. V. Kondratyuk, K. E. Tsiolkovskiy, F. A. Tsander
* **Title**: *Pioneers of Rocket Technology: Selected Works* (*Пионеры ракетной техники*)
* **Original Publisher**: Nauka Publishing House (Moscow, 1964)
* **Technical Translation**: NASA TT F-9285 / TT 65-50114 (Washington, D.C., 1965)
* **Call Number**: `VML-TL781.P56-1965`
* **LCC**: `TL781 .P56 1965` (History of Rocketry) / `TL782` (Rocket Technology Collections)
* **DDC**: `629.40947` (Astronautics · History)
* **Format**: Complete digital edition with 42 high-resolution engineering schematics and mathematical derivations.

### Editorial Committee & Pioneer Biographies

#### Nikolai Ivanovich Kibal'chich (1853–1881)
* **Biographical Sketch**: Russian revolutionary, chemist, and explosive engineer of Narodnaya Volya. Sentenced to death in 1881 for participating in the assassination of Tsar Alexander II.
* **Theoretical Contribution**: In his death cell days before execution, designed and calculated humanity's first manned rocket-propelled aircraft using slow-burning gunpowder cartridges, thrust vectoring, and pendulum stabilization (*Project for an Aeronautical Machine*).

#### Yuriy Vasil'yevich Kondratyuk (Aleksandr Ignat'yevich Shargey, 1897–1942)
* **Biographical Sketch**: One of the most visionary independent scientists of the 20th century. Worked as a mechanic and grain elevator engineer in Western Siberia, self-publishing his groundbreaking theories without academic patronage.
* **Theoretical Contribution**: Independently derived the rocket motion differential equations and pioneered:
  - **Lunar Orbit Rendezvous (LOR)**: Mothership remains in lunar orbit while a lightweight modular lander descends and docks upon return (the foundational architecture adopted by NASA for the Apollo missions).
  - **Gravity Assist (Gravitational Slingshot)**: Utilization of celestial gravitational fields for spacecraft acceleration and trajectory redirection.
  - **Atmospheric Glide Reentry**: Thermal protection via multi-layer shielding, aerodynamic lift braking, and parabolic trajectories.

#### Konstantin Eduardovich Tsiolkovskiy (1857–1935)
* **Biographical Sketch**: Father of theoretical astronautics and rocket dynamics. Overcame severe childhood deafness through self-study, serving as a mathematics teacher in Kaluga.
* **Theoretical Contribution**: Published *The Exploration of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices* (1903), formulated the fundamental **Tsiolkovskiy Rocket Equation**:
  $$v = c \cdot \ln\left(\frac{M_0}{M_k}\right)$$
  and conceptualized multistage rocket trains, space stations, and airlock life-support systems.

#### Fridrikh Arturovich Tsander (1887–1933)
* **Biographical Sketch**: Pioneer of early Soviet liquid-propellant rocket engines and co-founder of the Group for the Study of Reactive Motion (GIRD).
* **Theoretical Contribution**: Championed interplanetary travel with his motto "Forward to Mars!". Conceived burning the structural metallic parts of rockets (aluminum, magnesium alloys) as secondary fuel in flight, and successfully engineered the OR-1 and OR-2 liquid-fuel rocket engines.

#### Ivan Vsevolodovich Meshcherskiy (1859–1935)
* **Biographical Sketch**: Renowned Russian applied mathematician and Professor of Applied Mechanics at Saint Petersburg Polytechnic Institute.
* **Theoretical Contribution**: Founded the analytical mechanics of variable-mass bodies, formulating the universal **Meshcherskiy Differential Equation**:
  $$M \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = \vec{F} + \vec{u}_{rel} \frac{dM}{dt}$$
  serving as the rigorous mathematical cornerstone of all modern rocketry, jet propulsion, and orbital mechanics.

#### Dr. Viktor Nikolayevich Sokol'skiy (1914–1996)
* **Academic Standing**: Head of History of Science and Technology Section, USSR Academy of Sciences; Member of the International Academy of Astronautics (IAA).
* **Archival Contribution**: Led the forensic recovery, archival authentication, and critical editing of the original manuscripts of Kibal'chich and Kondratyuk, establishing theoretical priorities in world astronautical history.
