## Library Classification & Call Number System

Voile Magic Library utilizes the **Voile Magic Library Classification (VMLC)** system for digital rare books and special collections, fully cross-referenced with international standard library classifications:
* **Library of Congress Classification (LCC)**
* **Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC)**
* **Government Technical Report Series (NASA TT / DEA Codes)**
* **State Archival Record Group Numbers**

### Call Number Construction Formula

$$\text{VML-}[\text{LCC Subject \& Cutter Number}]\text{-}[\text{Filing Year}]$$

* **KF3890.S58**: United States federal controlled substances law & forensic regulation (Alexander T. Shulgin)
* **TL781.P56**: History of rocketry theory & pioneer astronautical treatises (Pioneers of Rocket Technology)

* **Foreword Contributors**: Ronald K. Siegel, Ph.D.; Gary L. Henderson, Ph.D.; David P. Krivitsky, Esq.
* **Digital Format**: Complete 10 chapters, 33 chemical scaffold plates, DEA code tables, and Hill system empirical formula index.

### Author & Contributor Biographies

#### Principal Author: Alexander "Sasha" Shulgin, Ph.D. (1925–2014)
* **Credentials**: Ph.D. in Biochemistry from UC Berkeley; world-renowned "Godfather of Psychedelics"; leading pioneer of psychopharmacology.
* **Background**: Served in the U.S. Navy during WWII. Senior Research Chemist at Dow Chemical, where he invented Zectran, the first commercially successful biodegradable pesticide.
* **Forensic Advisory**: Maintained a Schedule I DEA research license for decades; served as expert witness and technical advisor for DEA agents, prosecutors, and forensic scientists.

#### Foreword Contributor: Ronald K. Siegel, Ph.D.
* UCLA Neuropsychiatric Institute; renowned expert on hallucinogens, behavioral pharmacology, and forensic toxicology.

#### Foreword Contributor: Gary L. Henderson, Ph.D.
* UC Davis School of Medicine; foremost authority on fentanyl analogue pharmacokinetics and GC-MS forensic detection.

#### Foreword Contributor: David P. Krivitsky, Esq.
* California criminal defense attorney specializing in federal controlled substances litigation and statutory interpretation.
