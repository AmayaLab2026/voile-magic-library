# Chapter 7: Empirical Formula Index


> **Chapter Introduction (by Alexander T. Shulgin, Ph.D.)**:
>
> All controlled substances listed in Chapter 2, as well as all chemical materials tabulated in Table III of Chapter 5 (secondary drugs, organic reagents, solvents, and biochemical preparations), are compiled comprehensively in this index in order of their **empirical formula**, arranged according to the **Hill System** (carbon atoms $C$ first, followed by hydrogen atoms $H$, with all remaining elements arranged in alphabetical order).
>
> Consequently, **every chemical substance that has been assigned a Drug Control Number by the United States Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) can be located and cross-referenced in this index**. The chapter designation preceding the drug name indicates where detailed documentation appears in this reference work:
> * **Prefix 2**: Indicates that the drug is described in **Chapter 2** (statutorily scheduled drugs and their detailed regulatory status);
> * **Prefix 5**: Indicates that the substance is listed in **Chapter 5, Table III** (secondary drugs, biochemical reagents, or precursor solvents not individually scheduled).
>
> **Identification and Search Principle**: If a given chemical compound cannot be located in this empirical formula index, **it establishes conclusively that the substance had not been assigned an individual Drug Control Number in any publicly released DEA registry or control schedule as of April 1, 1991**.
