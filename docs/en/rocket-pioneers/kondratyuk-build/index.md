# Part II · To Whomsoever Will Read in Order to Build (Kondratyuk's First Manuscript)
### To Whomsoever Will Read in Order to Build (1918–1919)
**By Yuri Vasilievich Kondratyuk (Yu. V. Kondratyuk)**  
*Compiled and Annotated by the Institute of the History of Natural Science and Technology, Academy of Sciences of the USSR*

---

## Overview and Historical Context of the Manuscript

*To Whomsoever Will Read in Order to Build* (*Тем, кто будет читать, чтобы строить*) is the earliest surviving astronautical theoretical manuscript by the Soviet space pioneer **Yuri Vasilievich Kondratyuk** (real name: Aleksandr Ignat'yevich Shargey, 1897–1942). Originally drafted between 1918 and 1919 during the harrowing years of the Russian Civil War, the young author lived in obscurity in Poltava (Ukraine) and later on the Siberian frontier. In complete isolation from the scientific community, entirely lacking technical literature and laboratory instruments, Kondratyuk relied upon astonishing intuitive genius and rigorous classical mechanics to independently establish a comprehensive theoretical and technical framework for astrodynamics and interplanetary flight.

The manuscript was never published during Kondratyuk's lifetime, circulating only among family, close friends, and a handful of scholars. In 1938, Kondratyuk formally entrusted and mailed his original manuscripts to the Academy of Sciences of the USSR. Following the outbreak of the Great Patriotic War, Kondratyuk volunteered for front-line infantry service and fell heroically in battle during the defense of Moscow in 1942. In 1964, the Institute of the History of Natural Science and Technology of the Academy of Sciences of the USSR collected and edited this first manuscript in its complete form into *Pioneers of Rocket Technology: Selected Works*, which was subsequently translated into English as an official NASA publication (NASA TT F-9285).

## Milestones of Visionary Technological Foresight

In this manuscript, penned when humanity had not even constructed a functioning modern liquid-fueled rocket, Kondratyuk outlined with staggering prescience virtually all core pillars of human space exploration for the subsequent half-century:

1. **Equation of Rocket Motion and Gravity Loss Correction**: Independently derived the ideal rocket velocity equation (parallel to Tsiolkovsky) and pioneered the mathematical penalty factor ($1 + \frac{1}{k-1}$) in the logarithmic propellant exponent to account for the time integral of gravitational acceleration.
2. **Physiological Immersion Against High-g Overloads (Immersion Tank)**: Demonstrated from hydrostatic principles that immersing cosmonauts in closed containers filled with liquids of equal body density protects the human body against mechanical accelerations many times beyond conventional physiological thresholds.
3. **High-Energy Propellants and Ion Propulsion**: Systematically analyzed the immense calorific value of liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen, and innovatively proposed using electrical energy to accelerate atoms into cathode rays (electron/high-velocity ion streams) for reactive propulsion—the earliest genesis of modern electric and ion propulsion.
4. **Multistage Rocket Staging and Tank Jettisoning**: Proposed specific mechanical configurations for shedding spent propellant tanks along the flight trajectory to drastically reduce inert dead-weight ratio (i.e., multistage rocketry).
5. **Three-Axis Gyroscopic Inertial Stabilization**: Designed an attitude control system combining gimbaled sealed gyroscopes and servo steering rods to control the deflection of rocket exhaust nozzles.
6. **Aerodynamic Braking and Gliding Reentry into Planetary Atmospheres ("Eureka!")**: Made the epoch-making realization that returning to Earth does not require carrying an equivalent mass of retro-propellant; rather, by employing a winged lifting body to enter the rarefied planetary atmosphere, aerodynamic drag dissipates orbital kinetic energy gradually into heat, saving upwards of 90% of the spacecraft's launch mass.
7. **Space Solar Concentrators and Orbital Bases**: Conceived deploying lightweight thin-film solar mirrors in low Earth orbit to capture infinite radiant energy and establishing permanent manned orbital refueling and staging stations.
8. **Gravitational Slingshot (Gravity Assist) for Acceleration and Deceleration**: Proved via analytical geometry and celestial mechanics that a spacecraft flying past a moving celestial body (such as the Moon or planets) can "borrow" the body's orbital kinetic energy to accelerate or decelerate—the exact gravity-assist trajectories employed by contemporary deep-space probes (Voyager, Cassini, New Horizons).
9. **Electromagnetic Rail Launchers (Mass Drivers)**: Outlined launching space projectiles via multi-stage energized copper coils accelerating the vehicle along an extended track.

## Chapter Directory

This translation of Part II strictly adheres to the authoritative text in NASA TT F-9285 and the original manuscripts preserved in the Central State Archive of the October Revolution in Moscow, structured into the following sections:

* **[Section 1: Fundamental Principles of Reactive Propulsion and Ideal Velocity Equation](./section-1.md)** (pp. 18–22)  
  *Examines basic rules of space travel, physical limits of conventional cannons and slings, derivation of reactive thrust equations, Earth's gravitational potential, and physiological acceleration limits.*
* **[Section 2: Orbital Ascent, Physiological Protection, and Active Propellants](./section-2.md)** (pp. 23–27)  
  *Details the second method of flight (tangential ascent into orbit to counteract gravity), immersion tanks for high-g tolerance, thermochemical heats of reaction, and cathode-ray (ion) propulsion concepts.*
* **[Section 3: Spacecraft Design, Multistage Separation, and Gyroscopic Orientation](./section-3.md)** (pp. 28–33)  
  *Analyzes spacecraft structural cross-sections, tank stress under acceleration, step-by-step jettisoning of empty tanks (multistage rocketry), and three-axis gimbaled gyroscopic attitude controls.*
* **[Section 4: Nozzle Expansion, Celestial Orientation, and the Atmospheric Discovery](./section-4.md)** (pp. 34–38)  
  *Studies parabolic Laval expansion nozzle geometry, phase changes and regenerative cooling, periscopic celestial orientation, and the "Eureka!" realization of aerodynamic atmospheric braking.*
* **[Section 5: Gliding Reentry, Atmospheric Braking, and Thermal Protection](./section-5.md)** (pp. 39–41)  
  *Provides rigorous calculations of propellant consumption across three return regimes, inverted and winged glider reentry profiles, and hypersonic gliding attitude and thermal shielding.*
* **[Section 6: Space Solar Concentrators, Gravitational Slingshots, and Orbital Bases](./section-6.md)** (pp. 42–51)  
  *Investigates ultra-light space solar mirrors, satellite orbital supply bases, the celestial mechanics of gravity assists, and multistage electromagnetic launch cannons.*
* **[Editorial Commentary and Technical Notes](./commentary.md)** (pp. 52–59)  
  *Presents 28 exhaustive technical annotations, historical datings, dimensional analyses, thermodynamic verifications, and priority appraisals compiled by the Soviet Academy of Sciences editorial board.*
