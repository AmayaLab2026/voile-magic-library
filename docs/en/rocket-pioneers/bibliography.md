# Appendix · Comprehensive Archival Index and Bibliography
### Bibliography of the Published Works of N. I. Kibal'chich, K. E. Tsiolkovskiy, F. A. Tsander, and Yu. V. Kondratyuk on the Problems of Reactive Flying Machines and Interplanetary Travel
**Compiled by B. N. Vorob'yev**  
*(From NASA TT F-9285, pp. 159–165 / Russian edition pp. 669–678)*

---

## I. Nikolay Ivanovich Kibal'chich (N. I. Kibal'chich)

1. ***Project for an Aeronautical Machine*** (*Proyekt vozdukhoplavatel'nogo pribora*)  
   Published in *Byloye*, Nos. 10–11, pp. 115–121, 1918.
2. ***N. I. Kibal'chich's Project for an Aeronautical Machine***  
   In A. Shiukov: *"The 'Narodovolets' N. I. Kibal'chich and His Project for a Reactive Flying Machine (On the 48th Anniversary of Kibal'chich's Death)"* (*Narodovolets N. I. Kibal'chich i yego proyekt reaktivnogo letatel'nogo apparata*), published in *Vestnik Vozdukhnogo Flota* (*Air Fleet Herald*), No. 4, pp. 39–41, 1929.
3. ***Note of N. I. Kibal'chich Concerning the Reactive Flying Machine Proposed by Him*** (*Zapiska N. I. Kibal'chicha o predlozhennom im reaktivnom letatel'nom pribore*)  
   Included in the anthology *Aeronautics and Aviation in Russia Prior to 1907* (*Vozdukhoplavaniye i aviatsiya v Rossii do 1907*), Collection of Documents and Materials, edited by V. A. Popov. State Publishing House of Defense Industry (Oborongiz), Moscow, 1956, pp. 236–240.
4. ***The Case of March 1, 1881*** (*Delo 1-go marta 1881 g.*)  
   Official report of the Tsarist Government, St. Petersburg, 1906, pp. 328–329.  
   *Original manuscript archival reference*: Central State Archive of the October Revolution (TsGAOR), Police Department Archive (F. D. P.), 3rd Processing Office, 1881, Case 79, Part I.

## II. Constantine Eduardovich Tsiolkovskiy (K. E. Tsiolkovskiy) (1–61)

1. ***How to Protect Fragile and Delicate Objects from Jolts and Shocks*** (*Kak predokhranit' khrupkiye i nezhnyye veshchi ot tolchkov i udarov*)  
   *Trudy otdeleniya fizicheskikh nauk Obshchestva lyubiteley yestestvoznaniya* (*Transactions of the Physics Section of the Society of Friends of Natural Science*), Moscow, Vol. 4, No. 2, pp. 17–18, 1891.
2. ***On the Moon: A Science-Fiction Tale*** (*Na Lune. Fantasticheskaya povest'*)  
   Izd. Sytina, Moscow, 1893. 48 pp., 4 illustrations.
3. ***Daydreams of the Earth and the Sky and the Effects of Universal Gravitation*** (*Grezy o Zemle i nebe i effekty vsemirnogo tyagoteniya*)  
   Izd. A. N. Goncharova, Moscow, 1895. 143 pp., unillustrated.
4. ***Can the Earth Ever Notify the Inhabitants of Other Planets of the Existence on It of Intelligent Beings?*** (*Mozhet li kogda-nibud' Zemlya zayavit' zhitelyam drugikh planet o sushchestvovanii na ney razumnykh sushchestv?*)  
   *Kaluzhskiy Vestnik* newspaper, No. 68, November 26, 1896.
5. ***Investigation of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices*** (*Issledovaniye mirovykh prostranstv reaktivnymi priborami*)  
   *Nauchnoye Obozreniye*, St. Petersburg, No. 5, pp. 45–75, 1903. (**Foundational work of astronautics; first derivation of the Tsiolkovskiy rocket equation**).
6. ***A Reactive Device as a Means of Flight in a Vacuum and in the Atmosphere*** (*Reaktivnyy pribor, kak sredstvo poleta v pustote i atmosfere*)  
   *Vozdukhoplavatel'* (*The Aeronaut*), No. 2, pp. 110–113, 1910.
7. ***Investigation of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices (Continuation)*** (*Issledovaniye mirovykh prostranstv reaktivnymi priborami*)  
   *Vestnik Vozdukhoplavaniya*, 1911, Nos. 19–22; 1912, Nos. 2, 3, 5–7, 9.
8. ***Without Gravity: A Science-Fiction Narrative*** (*Bez tyazhesti. Nauchno-fantasticheskiy rasskaz*)  
   *Priroda i Lyudi* (*Nature and Men*), No. 36, pp. 577–579, 1914.
9. ***Investigation of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices: Supplement to Parts I and II of the Work of the Same Name*** (*Issledovaniye mirovykh prostranstv reaktivnymi priborami: dopolneniye k I i II chasti odnoimennogo truda*)  
   Published by the author, Kaluga, 1914. 16 pp., 1 illustration.
10. ***Beyond the Planet Earth*** (*Vne Zemli*, science-fiction novel)  
    Published by the Kaluga Society for the Study of Nature and Local Lore, Kaluga, 1920. 118 pp.
11. ***The Rocket into Cosmic Space*** (*Raketa v kosmicheskoye prostranstvo*)  
    Second edition. Kaluga, 1924. 32 pp., 1 illustration.
12. ***Letter to the Editor*** (*Pis'mo v redaktsiyu*)  
    *Svyaz'* (*Communications*), No. 18, p. 14, 1925. With portrait of the author.
13. ***Investigation of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices*** (*Issledovaniye mirovykh prostranstv reaktivnymi priborami*)  
    Revised reprint of the 1903 and 1911 articles, with additions and corrections. Kaluga, 1926. 127 pp., 3 illustrations.
14. ***The Cosmic Rocket: Experimental Preparation*** (*Kosmicheskaya raketa. Opytnaya podgotovka*)  
    Kaluga, 1927. 24 pp., 1 illustration.
15. ***Cosmic Rocket Trains*** (*Kosmicheskiye raketnyye poyezda*)  
    With a biographical sketch of Tsiolkovskiy by S. V. Bessonov entitled *"The Reaction Engine"* (*Reaktivnyy dvigatel'*). Published by the Kaluga Section of Scientific Workers, 1929. 38 pp., 3 illustrations, portrait of Tsiolkovskiy. (**First monograph on multistage rocketry**).
16. ***On the Moon: A Science-Fiction Tale*** (*Na Lune. Fantasticheskaya povest'*)  
    Foreword by Ya. I. Perel'man. Gosizdat, Moscow-Leningrad, 1929. 64 pp., 8 illustrations.
17. ***The New Airplane; Beyond the Earth's Atmosphere; The Reaction Engine*** (*Novyy aeroplan. Za atmosferoy Zemli. Reaktivnyy dvigatel'*)  
    Kaluga, 1929. 38 pp., 4 illustrations.

    Kaluga, 1929. 40 pp.

    Published by the author, Kaluga, 1930. 24 pp.
21. ***On the Moon: A Science-Fiction Tale*** (*Na Misyatsi. Fantastichna povist'*)  
    Foreword by Ya. I. Perel'man, Ukrainian edition. Kharkov, 1930. 48 pp., 7 illustrations.

    *V Boy za Tekhniku* (*Battle for Technology*), Nos. 15–16, pp. 19–21, 1932. 3 illustrations.

## III. Fridrikh Arturovich Tsander (F. A. Tsander) (1–10)

1. ***Flights to Other Planets*** (*Perelety na drugiye planety*)  
   *Tekhnika i Zhizn'*, No. 13, pp. 15–16, 1924.
2. ***Reaction Engines*** (*Reaktivnyye dvigateli*)  
   *Samolet*, No. 1, pp. 31–32, 1932.
3. ***Problems of Flight by Means of Reactive Machines*** (*Problema poleta pri pomoshchi reaktivnykh apparatov*)  
   State Publishing House of Aviation and Automotive Industry (Gosaviavtoizdat), Moscow, 1932. 75 pp., 36 plates. (**Tsander's principal monograph published during his lifetime**).
4. ***Thermal Calculation of a Liquid-Propellant Rocket Engine*** (*Teplovoy raschet raketnogo dvigatelya na zhidkom toplive*)  
   In the symposium *Raketnaya tekhnika* (*Rocket Technology*), edited jointly by I. T. Kleymenov, G. E. Langemak, M. K. Tikhonravov, S. P. Korolev, and Yu. A. Pobedonostsev. Moscow-Leningrad, 1936, No. 1, pp. 97–120.
5. ***Application of Metallic Fuel in Rocket Engines*** (*Primeneniye metallicheskogo topliva v raketnykh dvigatelyakh*)  
   In the symposium *Raketnaya tekhnika*, Moscow-Leningrad, 1936, No. 1, pp. 121–135.
6. ***Thermal Calculation of a Liquid-Propellant Rocket Engine (Continuation)***  
   In the symposium *Raketnaya tekhnika*, Moscow-Leningrad, 1937, No. 5, pp. 77–90.
7. ***Design Problems of a Rocket Utilizing Metallic Fuel*** (*Voprosy konstruirovaniya rakety, ispol'zuyushchey metallicheskoye toplivo*)  
   In the symposium *Raketnaya tekhnika*, Moscow-Leningrad, 1937, No. 5, pp. 91–110.
8. ***Comparison of Fuel Consumption for the Case Where Oxygen Is Taken from the Atmosphere and the Case Where It Is Stored in the Rocket*** (*Sravneniye raskhoda topliva dlya sluchaya, kogda kislorod beretsya iz atmosfery, i dlya sluchaya, kogda on zapasen v rakete*)  
   In the symposium *Raketnaya tekhnika*, Moscow-Leningrad, 1937, No. 5, pp. 111–115.
9. ***Problems of Flight by Means of Reactive Machines*** (*Problema poleta pri pomoshchi reaktivnykh apparatov*)  
   Collection of works, edited by M. K. Tikhonravov. Oborongiz, Moscow, 1947. 240 pp.
10. ***Problems of Flight by Means of Reactive Machines: Interplanetary Flights*** (*Problema poleta pri pomoshchi reaktivnykh apparatov. Mezhplanetnyye polety*)  
    Collection of works, edited with a foreword by L. K. Korneyev. Second enlarged edition. Oborongiz, Moscow, 1961. 459 pp.

## IV. Yuriy Vasil'yevich Kondratyuk (Yu. V. Kondratyuk) (1–3)

1. ***To Whoever Will Read to Build*** (*Tem, kto budet chitat', chtoby stroit'*)  
   Scientific manuscript. Permanently preserved in the Institute for the History of Natural Science and Technology, Academy of Sciences of the USSR. Signed holograph (1918–1919). 144 pp. **Published in full for the first time in this collection** (see pp. 18–51 of this volume).
2. ***Conquest of Interplanetary Space*** (*Zavoyevaniye mezhplanetnykh prostranstv*)  
   Reviewed with a foreword by Prof. V. P. Vetchinkin. Published by the author, Novosibirsk, 1929. 72 pp., with 6 large folding plates.
3. ***Conquest of Interplanetary Space*** (Second edition)  
   Edited by P. I. Ivanov. Oborongiz, Moscow, 1947. 82 pp., 7 illustrations.
