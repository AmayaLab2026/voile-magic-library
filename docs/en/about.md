# About the Library: Voile Magic Library
### *Welcome to Voile Magic Library*

> *"Yellowed pages whispering of sandalwood and ink; the quiet light of human reason rests in silence here."*

---

## 🏛️ What Kind of Library Is This?

**Voile Magic Library** (ヴワル魔法図書館) was originally named after the subterranean grand library buried deep beneath the Scarlet Devil Mansion in Gensokyo. Watched over by witches and scholars, myriad ancient tomes and manuscripts holding the fundamental secrets of the world sleep in peace.

In modern digital gardens, this library was created by **AmayaLab** and is maintained by head librarian **Reina Neko** ([@YuukiReina2023](https://github.com/YuukiReina2023)), serving as an **open-source digital archive dedicated to hard-core cross-disciplinary scholarship and frontier forbidden knowledge**.

Rather than following superficial trends, we focus on profound literature bridging **advanced organic chemistry, psychopharmacology, forensic toxicology, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and legal regulatory boundaries**.

---

## 🔮 Collection Philosophy

1. **Forbidden Knowledge with the Scent of Books**:  
   We curate authoritative treatises exploring the boundaries of human cognition, receptor-ligand interactions, chemical scaffold derivatizations, and legal regulatory evolution.
2. **Rigorous Scientific Rationality**:  
   We stand firmly on modern molecular orbital theory, structure-activity relationships (SAR), and authoritative legal statutory jurisprudence.
3. **Elegant Reading Aesthetics**:  
   Adhering to Claude-inspired warm paper tones, rounded pill-shaped controls, and classical serif typography, every study session feels like turning pages under a soft desk lamp.
