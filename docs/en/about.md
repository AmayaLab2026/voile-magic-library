# About the Library: Voile Magic Library
### Welcome to Voile Magic Library

> *"Yellowed pages whispering of sandalwood and ink; the quiet light of human reason rests in silence here."*

---

## What Kind of Library Is This?

**Voile Magic Library** (ヴワル魔法図書館) was originally named after the subterranean grand library buried deep beneath the Scarlet Devil Mansion in Gensokyo. Watched over by witches and scholars, myriad ancient tomes and manuscripts holding the fundamental secrets of the world sleep in peace.

In modern digital gardens, this library was created by **AmayaLab** and is maintained by head librarian **Reina Neko** ([@YuukiReina2023](https://github.com/YuukiReina2023)), serving as an **open-source digital archive dedicated to hard-core cross-disciplinary scholarship and frontier forbidden knowledge**.

Rather than following superficial trends, we focus on profound literature bridging **advanced organic chemistry, psychopharmacology, forensic toxicology, theoretical astrodynamics and rocketry history, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and legal regulatory boundaries**.

---

## Collection Philosophy

1. **Forbidden Knowledge with the Scent of Books**:  
   We curate authoritative treatises exploring the boundaries of human cognition, receptor-ligand interactions, chemical scaffold derivatizations, deep-space astronautical mechanics, and legal regulatory evolution.
2. **Rigorous Scientific Rationality**:  
   We stand firmly on modern molecular orbital theory, rigorous variable-mass analytical mechanics, and authoritative statutory jurisprudence, laying bare the logic behind every chemical scaffold, orbital derivation, and legal code.
3. **Elegant Reading Aesthetics**:  
   Adhering to Claude-inspired warm paper tones, rounded pill-shaped controls, and classical serif typography, every study session feels like turning pages under a soft desk lamp.

**Controlled Substances: A Chemical and Legal Guide to the Federal Drug Laws**  
*Authored by: Alexander T. Shulgin, Ph.D. (2nd Edition, 1992)*

- **Volume**: A 436-page landmark work compiled by legendary medicinal chemist and toxicologist Dr. Alexander "Sasha" Shulgin.

### [Pioneers of Rocket Technology: Selected Works](/rocket-pioneers/)
**Pioneers of Rocket Technology: Selected Works**  
*Compiled by the Institute of the History of Natural Science and Technology, USSR Academy of Sciences · NASA TT F-9285 (Chinese Edition)*

- **Volume**: Landmark historical treatises and declassified manuscripts by Russia and the USSR's four founding rocket pioneers (1881–1933).
- **Significance**: Includes Nikolai Kibal'chich's 1881 death-cell manuscript on pulse combustion propulsion, Yuriy Kondratyuk's works formulating Lunar Orbit Rendezvous (LOR) and gravitational slingshots (*To Whom Will Read to Build*, 1919; *Conquest of Interplanetary Space*, 1929), and Sokol'skiy's historical survey; features 42 engineering plates.
